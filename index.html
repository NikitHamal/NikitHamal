Nik 0.1

A tiny Romanized-Nepali & English LLM built from scratch that thinks before answering and runs 100% inside your browser.

Features a custom bilingual tokenizer, hybrid autoregressive + diffusion transformer architecture, explicit step-by-step column-borrow reasoning traces, and a hand-tuned WASM-SIMD kernel. Validated across 2,304 fresh NikBench evaluation items.

२ · दर्शन र यात्रा Philosophy & Lineage

How code and self-inquiry meet.

Whether architecting a distributed backend or observing thought in silence, the underlying discipline is the same: strip away the unnecessary until only what is essential remains.

०१ · अद्वैत (Advaita)

Non-Dual Architecture

In great systems, interface and engine are not two separate things bolted together—the clarity of the data model naturally surfaces as the calm of the UI.

०२ · साक्षी (Sākṣī)

Unclouded Observation

Debugging complex state and examining human conditioning require the exact same posture: look directly at what is actually happening, without projection.

०३ · सहज (Sahaja)

Effortless Restraint

“Effort clouds the effortless.” Over-engineered abstractions and noisy interfaces hide insecurity; true craftsmanship feels quiet, light, and inevitable.

०४ · कल्याण (Kalyāṇa)

Tools That Serve & Step Back

Software should solve the human problem in front of it—a student preparing for exams, two families connecting—and never hijack their attention.

One question led to the next.
