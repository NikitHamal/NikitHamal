About Me

I'm Nikit Hamal, an 18-year-old creative technologist, writer, and philosopher. As a multidisciplinary creator, I bridge the worlds of technology and art to build meaningful digital experiences.

My journey began with a curiosity about how things work, evolving into a passion for crafting elegant solutions to complex problems. I specialize in web and Android development, with a keen eye for UI/UX design and a love for creating things that are not only functional but also beautiful and intuitive.

Beyond code, I explore the intersection of technology and humanity, pondering philosophical questions through poetry and prose. I believe technology should serve humanity, and I strive to create work that reflects that belief.
