Core Principles · Ethos

The architecture of mind & machinery

Software engineering and philosophical contemplation are not opposing pursuits. Both demand rigorous observation of assumptions, elimination of friction, and reverence for fundamental truth.

01 · ADVAITA Non-Duality

Boundaries are Created by Thought

Eliminating artificial separation between data and state, client and edge. True simplicity arrives when accidental abstractions are dissolved.

02 · UPADHI Native Precision

Deep Respect for the Metal

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, WASM-SIMD kernels, and zero runtime bloat. Software should honor the hardware and battery life of the person holding it.

03 · NIRVICHARA Stillness

Clarity Precedes Syntax

Code written from quiet attention carries enduring elegance. When compulsive velocity is replaced with clarity, bugs vanish before they are written.

04 · KALYANA Living Tools

Dignify Human Attention

Building apps like NEBians and Lami Nepal that solve real human challenges without relying on addictive dopamine loops or attention theft.
