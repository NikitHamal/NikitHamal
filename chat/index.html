AR = one token per step through a hand-written WASM-SIMD int8 kernel (fastest on CPU). SSBD = self-speculative block diffusion: Nik drafts a block in parallel with its diffusion head and verifies it with its own AR head. Same output distribution and fewer sequential steps, which pays off on GPUs (experimental here).
