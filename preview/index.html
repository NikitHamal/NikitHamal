painted in code

Me, from baby to now, one brushstroke at a time.

These aren't photos or AI images. A painting algorithm I wrote studied my photos and rebuilt each face as tens of thousands of brushstrokes. Your browser paints every stroke live. Each new age is painted straight over the last, so you watch the face grow up.

Time-lapses

04 — how

How it's made

  1. Cut each photo out from its background.
  2. Blur the photo at several sizes, from very soft to sharp.
  3. Wherever the canvas still differs from the photo, place a stroke. The stroke follows the direction of the hair, skin and fabric, and stops when the colour changes.
  4. Go from big brushes to tiny ones, packing all the strokes into a small binary file of numbers.
  5. Your browser reads those numbers and paints them onto the canvas.
