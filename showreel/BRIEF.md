# Showreel — "Drawn in code"
Spec: 1920x1080@60, 15 s (900 f), 120 BPM (crisp pace — a showreel asks for energy), audio: code-made score + SFX, master -14 LUFS, TP <= -1.5 dBTP.
Format: launch film / teaser row (shortened). Engine: HTML canvas frame-function rendered headless (HyperFrames-style), muxed with ffmpeg.
Subject: Nikit Hamal — the portraits, pencil drawings, pixel scenes and mascot made in code on /preview.
Shown: portfolio, social, résumé link.

Brand (supplied by the portfolio; overrides cinetic defaults): paper #f3eee6 (cream), ink #211a16, accent #b4553c (terracotta),
type Plus Jakarta Sans (the portfolio's sans). The cream paper and terracotta accent are brand-supplied exceptions to the colour bans.

Hard bans (QA contract): no eyebrow/kicker labels, no stacked taglines, no "Introducing", copy in 1–4 word fragments only,
no serif/italic, no neon/glow/bloom, no purple or multi-hue gradients, no glassmorphism, no emoji/stock icons, no confetti/particle
decoration, no lens flares or glitch transitions, no overshoot on copy, no crossfade through black.
