## Facts and how they are worded
- **Devi Mahatmya** is chapters 81–93 of the Markandeya Purana, usually dated to about the 5th–6th century CE, so "about 1,500 years". Durga is formed from the combined *tejas* of the gods because Mahishasura could not be killed by a god or a man.
- **Nine nights → tenth day.** The nine-night battle is the festival tradition (Navaratri), not a statement in the text, so line 6 says *परम्पराअनुसार*.
- **Rama.** The Ravana victory is joined to Vijaya Dashami in popular tradition, so line 7 says *जोडिन्छ*.
- **Taleju Bhawani** is the lineage goddess of the Malla kings. Her temple at Hanuman Dhoka opens to the public once a year, on Mahanavami.
- **Fulpati.** After taking the Valley in 1825 BS (1768 CE), Prithvi Narayan Shah is credited with bringing the Fulpati from the Dashain Ghar at Gorkha to Hanuman Dhoka. Because this is a recorded tradition rather than a dated document, the line says *मानिन्छ*. The Nepali Army's Fulpati salute at Tundikhel continues today. The navapatrika is nine plants: banana, colocasia, turmeric, jayanti, bel, pomegranate, ashoka, manaka and paddy.
- **Days.** 1 Ghatasthapana; 7 Fulpati; 8 Maha Ashtami (Kalaratri); 9 Maha Navami (worship of weapons, tools and vehicles; Taleju opens); 10 Vijaya Dashami (tika and jamara from elders); 15 Kojagrat Purnima (Lakshmi asks *को जागर्ति*, "who is awake?").
- **2083 dates.** Ghatasthapana is Ashwin 25 (11 Oct 2026). Fulpati is Ashwin 31 (17 Oct). Tika is Kartik 4 (21 Oct), at 10:26. They are not shown on screen, so the film stays true in any year apart from its last card.
- **Plurality.** Not every community in Nepal celebrates Dashain, or celebrates it the same way. Line 19 says this plainly.
- **The blessing** (line 20) is the opening of the traditional tika shloka: *आयुर्द्रोणसुते श्रियो दशरथे शत्रुक्षयं राघवे ऐश्वर्यं नहुषे गतिश्च पवने मानं च दुर्योधने…*
