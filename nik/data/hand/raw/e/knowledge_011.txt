N: mustang ra jumla dubai syau ko lagi prasiddha chhan, ra mustang ko marpha syau ko utpadan ko lagi dherai chinchha. kun lai "bhandar" bhanne bhanne aadhikarik naam bhane chhaina.

### knowledge
U: pokhara ma begnas ra rupa tal kun nagarpalika ma parchhan?
N: begnas ra rupa tal pokhara mahanagarpalika kai bhag ma parchhan. yi tal sahar ko purbi bhag ma chhan.

### knowledge
U: devi's fall ko nepali naam k ho?
N: devi's fall lai nepali ma patale chhango bhaninchha. pokhara ko yo jharna ko pani jamin muni harauchha.

N: swayambhu ko purbi bato ma lamo dhunga ko sidhi chha, jaslai dherai le 365 sidhi bhanchhan. yo bato chadhda bandar haru dherai dekhinchhan.

### knowledge
U: swayambhu ko stupa ma k chitra hunchha?
N: swayambhu ko stupa ko charai tira buddha ka aankha kopieka chhan. bich ma naak jasto dekhine chinha nepali ank 1 jasto chha, jasle ekata janauchha bhanchhan.

### knowledge
U: bauddha stupa ma lhosar ma k hunchha?
N: lhosar ma bauddha stupa ma hajarau manchhe jamma hunchhan, puja garchhan, diyo balchhan ra paramparik naach gaan garchhan. stupa ko waripari kora (parikrama) garne chalan chha.

### knowledge
U: kora bhaneko k ho?
N: kora bhaneko stupa ya gumba ko waripari ghadi ko suiro ghumne disha ma ghumdai prarthana garne bauddha parampara ho. bauddhanath ra swayambhu ma kora garne manchhe dherai dekhinchhan.

### knowledge

N: maane ma "om mani padme hum" jasta mantra lekhieka hunchhan. bauddha bishwas anusar maane ghumauda mantra padhe jasto punya milchha.
