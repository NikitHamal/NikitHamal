N: The smallest bone is the stapes, in the middle ear. It helps carry sound to the inner ear.

### knowledge
U: what are the five basic tastes?
N: The 5 basic tastes are sweet, sour, salty, bitter and umami. Spicy heat is a feeling of burning, not a basic taste.

### knowledge
U: aankha ma kun bhag le prakash lai focus garchha?
N: aankha ma lens le prakash lai focus garchha. lens le retina ma sapha chitra banauna maddat garchha.

### knowledge
U: dimag kati gahrungo hunchha?
N: thulo manchhe ko dimag lagbhag 1.3 dekhi 1.4 kilo gahrungo hunchha. sharir ko tulna ma saano bhaye pani yasle dherai shakti khanchha.

### knowledge
U: sharir ma sabai bhanda dherai shakti kun ang le khanchha?
N: aaram ma basda sharir le khane shakti ko lagbhag 20 pratishat dimag le prayog garchha. dimag sharir ko saano bhag bhaye pani dherai shakti chahine ang ho.

### knowledge
U: mamsapeshi kati prakar ka hunchhan?
N: mamsapeshi 3 prakar ka hunchhan: haddi sanga jodiyeka (chalaauna sakine), mutu ko mamsapeshi ra pet-aandra jasta bhitri ang ko mamsapeshi. mutu ko mamsapeshi kahilyai nathaki chaliraha hunchha.

### knowledge
U: ragat kina rato hunchha?
N: ragat ko rato rang rato ragat koshika ma bhayeko haemoglobin le dinchha. haemoglobin ma falam (iron) hunchha, jasle oxygen samatchha.

### knowledge
U: shira niilo dekhinchha, ke ragat niilo hunchha?
N: hudaina, manchhe ko ragat sadhai rato hunchha. shira chhala muni bata herda niilo dekhinchha kinabhane prakash chhala ma pasdaa rang farak dekhinchha.

U: haai kina aauchha?
N: haai kina aauchha bhanne kura bigyan ma ahile pani pura thaha chhaina. thakai, nidra ra arulai haai garda dekhda dherai aauchha bhanne chahi thaha chha.
