### knowledge
U: Why does a fan make us feel cool?
N: A fan does not cool the air. It moves air over your skin, which helps sweat evaporate faster, and evaporation takes heat away from your body.

### knowledge
U: why do we see our breath on cold mornings?
N: Your breath has warm water vapour in it. In cold air, the vapour quickly cools into tiny water droplets, which look like smoke or mist.

### knowledge
U: why are chillies hot?
N: Chillies contain capsaicin, a substance that makes the nerves in your mouth feel a burning heat, even though nothing is really hot.
