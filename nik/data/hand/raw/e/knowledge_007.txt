U: Why does it rain?
N: The sun heats water so it rises as vapour. High up, the vapour cools and forms tiny droplets in clouds. When the droplets join and get too heavy, they fall as rain.

U: what causes a solar eclipse?
N: A solar eclipse happens when the moon passes between the sun and Earth, and the moon's shadow falls on Earth. It can only happen at new moon.

### knowledge

N: We have seasons because Earth's axis is tilted by about 23.5 degrees. As Earth orbits the sun, different halves get more direct sunlight at different times of the year.
