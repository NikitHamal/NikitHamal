### knowledge
U: madhumeh (diabetes) bhaneko k ho?
N: madhumeh bhaneko ragat ma chini (glucose) ko matra lamo samaya samma badhi rahane rog ho. sharir le insulin ramrari banauna ya prayog garna nasakda yo hunchha.

### knowledge
U: madhumeh ka lakshan k k hun?
N: madhumeh ka sadharan lakshan: dherai tirkha lagne, dherai pisab aaune, thakai, ghau dhilo niko hune ra kaaran bina tauil ghatne. yasto bhaye ragat ko chini jaanch garaunus.

### knowledge
U: type 1 ra type 2 madhumeh ma k farak chha?
N: type 1 ma sharir le insulin banauna nai sakdaina, yo dherai jaso saano umer ma dekhinchha. type 2 ma insulin ramrari kaam gardaina, yo badhi tauil, kam byayam ra umer sanga dherai jodiyeko hunchha.

### knowledge
U: madhumeh hunabata kasari bachne?
N: santulit khana, chini ra maida kam, niyamit byayam ra tauil niyantran garda type 2 madhumeh ko khatara dherai ghatchha. 40 barsa pachhi niyamit jaanch garaunu ramro hunchha.

### knowledge
U: khali pet ma ragat ko chini kati hunu samanya ho?
N: khali pet ma ragat ko chini lagbhag 70 dekhi 100 mg/dL samanya maninchha. duita jaanch ma 126 ya badhi aayo bhane doctor le madhumeh bhanna sakchhan.

### knowledge
U: uchcha raktachap lai "silent killer" kina bhanchhan?
N: uchcha raktachap le dherai jaso kunai lakshan dekhaudaina, tara bistarai mutu, mirgaula ra dimag lai noksan garchha. tyasaile lakshan nabhaye pani niyamit BP jaanch garnu parchha.

### knowledge
U: pressure (BP) ghataauna k garne?
N: khana ma nun kam garnus, niyamit hidnus, chhurot ra raksi chhodnus ra tauil milaunus. doctor le aushadhi diyeko bhaye niyamit khanus, aafai nachhodnus.

### knowledge
U: heart attack ka lakshan k hun?
N: heart attack ma chhati ma thichieko jasto dukhai hunchha, jun haat, ghaanti ya bangara samma failina sakchha. pasina aaune ra sas pherna garo hune pani hunchha; yasto bhaye turuntai ambulance bolaunus.

### knowledge
U: stroke (pakshaghat) ka lakshan kasari chinne?
N: stroke chinna FAST samjhanus: Face (anuhar ek tira dhalkinu), Arm (eutai haat kamjor), Speech (bolai laraborne) ra Time (turuntai aspatal). jati chhito upachar, tyati ramro.

### knowledge
U: cancer bhaneko k ho?
N: cancer bhaneko sharir ka koshika niyantran bina badhera gaanth banaune ra aru ang ma failina sakne rog ho. chadai pattaa lagaye dherai cancer ko upachar sambhab hunchha.

### knowledge
U: chhurot le kun kun cancer lagauna sakchha?
N: chhurot le phokso, mukh, ghaanti, khana nali ra pisab thaili ko cancer ko khatara badhauchha. chhurot chhodda jun sukai umer ma pani swasthya ma fayada hunchha.

### knowledge
U: HIV kasari sarchha?
N: HIV asurakshit yon samparka, sankramit ragat, eutai suiro ko prayog ra sankramit aama bata bachcha ma sarna sakchha. condom ko prayog ra naya suiro le khatara ghatauchha.

### knowledge
U: HIV kun kura bata sardaina?
N: HIV haat milauda, angalo halda, sangai khana khada, eutai charpi prayog garda ra laamkhutte le tokda sardaina. tyasaile HIV bhayeka manchhe sanga bhedbhav garnu hudaina.

### knowledge
U: HIV ra AIDS ma k farak chha?
N: HIV euta virus ho, jasle sharir ko rog sanga ladne shakti kamjor banauchha. upachar nagare lamo samaya pachhi yasle AIDS bhanne gambhir awastha lyauchha; aushadhi le HIV bhayeka manchhe lamo ra swastha jiwan bachna sakchhan.

### knowledge
U: hepatitis B bata kasari bachne?
N: hepatitis B bata bachna sabai bhanda ramro upaya khop ho. yo virus ragat, asurakshit yon samparka ra aama bata bachcha ma sarchha, ra liver lai noksan garchha.

### knowledge
U: kamalpitta (jaundice) bhaneko k ho?
N: kamalpitta ma ragat ma bilirubin badhera chhala ra aankha ko seto bhag pahelo hunchha. yo dherai jaso liver ko samasya ko lakshan ho, tyasaile doctor lai dekhaunu parchha.

### knowledge
U: raktaalpata (anemia) bhaneko k ho?
N: raktaalpata bhaneko ragat ma haemoglobin kam hunu ho, jasle sharir ma oxygen kam pugchha. thakai, anuhar fusro hune ra chhitai sas fulne yasko lakshan hun; falam (iron) bhayeko khana le maddat garchha.

### knowledge
U: gaand (goiter) kina aauchha?
N: khana ma iodine ko kami bhaye ghaanti ko thyroid granthi sunnera gaand aauchha. iodine yukta nun khane bani le nepal ma gaand dherai ghateko chha.

### knowledge
U: ratandho kun bhitamin ko kami le hunchha?
N: ratandho (raati ramro nadekhne) vitamin A ko kami le hunchha. gajar, farsi, hariyo saag ra pahelo phalphul ma vitamin A paainchha.

### knowledge
U: scurvy rog k ho?
N: scurvy vitamin C ko kami le hune rog ho, jasma gija bata ragat aaune, ghau dhilo niko hune ra thakai lagne hunchha. amilo phal, amala ra hariyo tarkari le yasbata bachauchha.

### knowledge
U: rickets rog kina hunchha?
N: rickets bachcha ma vitamin D ra calcium ko kami le hune rog ho, jasle haddi kamjor ra khutta bango banauchha. bihana ko ghaam ra dudh jasta khana le yasbata bachauna maddat garchha.

### knowledge
U: kwashiorkor bhaneko k ho?
N: kwashiorkor saano bachcha ma protein ko dherai kami le hune kuposhan ho. yasma pet phulne, khutta sunnine ra kapal ko rang badalinne hunchha.

### knowledge
U: nimonia (pneumonia) bhaneko k ho?
N: nimonia phokso ko sankraman ho, jasma khoki, jworo ra sas ferna garo hunchha. saano bachcha ra budha-budhi lai yo khatarnak huna sakchha, tyasaile chhito doctor kahan janu parchha.

### knowledge
U: jhada-pakhala lageko bachcha lai k garne?
N: bachcha lai jeevan jal (ORS) ra jhol kura dherai dinus, ra khana ra aama ko dudh nachhodnus. doctor ko salah anusar zinc chakki pani dinchhan; bachcha dherai sust bhaye ya ragat dekhiye turuntai aspatal laijaanus.

### knowledge
U: pet ma juka kasari parchha?
N: fohor haat le khana khada ra ramrari nadhoyeko tarkari khada juka ka anda pet ma pugchhan, ra khali khutta hidda kehi juka (jastai hookworm) ka saana lerva chhala bata pasna sakchhan. doctor ko salah ma juka marne aushadhi khanu ra sarsafai rakhnu parchha.

### knowledge
U: luto (scabies) kasari sarchha?
N: luto ekdam saano kira (mite) le lagaune chilaune rog ho, jun sangai sutda ra luga share garda sarchha. ghar ka sabai le sangai aushadhi lagaunu ra luga-ochhyan umalera dhunu parchha.

### knowledge
U: aankha pakyo (conjunctivitis) bhane k garne?
N: aankha pakda aankha rato hunchha, kachera aauchha ra chilauchha. aankha nachhunus, haat dhoirahanus, rumal share nagarnus, ra dherai bhaye doctor lai dekhaunus.

### knowledge
U: theula (chickenpox) k ho?
N: theula virus le lagaune rog ho, jasma jworo aauchha ra jiu bhari pani bhareko chilaune phoka niskinchhan. yo sajilai sarchha, tyasaile niko nahunjel school najanu ramro hunchha.

### knowledge
U: dadura (measles) bata kasari bachne?
N: dadura bata bachna khop (MR khop) nai sabai bhanda ramro upaya ho. yo dherai sajilai sarne virus ko rog ho, jasma jworo ra jiu bhari rato daana aauchha.

### knowledge
U: nepal polio mukta kahile bhayo?
N: nepal sahit dakshin-purbi asia kshetra lai 2014 ma WHO le polio mukta ghoshana garyo. polio bata bachauna bachcha lai polio ko thopa ra khop diine gariyeko chha.

### knowledge
U: kushtha rog niko hunchha?
N: hunchha, kushtha rog (leprosy) bacteria le lagaune rog ho ra aushadhi (MDT) le pura niko hunchha. yo sajilai sardaina, tyasaile birami sanga bhedbhav garnu hudaina.

### knowledge
U: kala-azar kasari sarchha?
N: kala-azar baluwa jhinga (sandfly) le tokda sarne rog ho. yasma lamo samaya jworo aaune, tauil ghatne ra phiyo badhne hunchha, ra nepal ko terai ma kahi kahi dekhinchha.

### knowledge
U: japanese encephalitis kasari lagchha?
N: japanese encephalitis laamkhutte le sarne virus ko rog ho, jasle dimag ma sujan lyauna sakchha. terai ma barsha ko bela badhi dekhinchha ra yasko khop paainchha.

### knowledge
U: dhanustankar (tetanus) kasari lagchha?
N: dhanustankar mato, gobar ya fohor kila jasta saman le baneko ghau bata bacteria pasera lagchha. yasle maansapeshi kasine gambhir rog lyauchha, tara khop le bachauna sakinchha.

### knowledge
U: bachcha lai BCG khop kina dinchhan?
N: BCG khop le kshayarog (TB) bata, bishesh gari bachcha lai lagne gambhir TB bata bachauchha. yo khop janma pachhi chadai diinchha.

### knowledge
U: khop le kasari kaam garchha?
N: khop le sharir lai rog ko kamjor ya mareko kitanu jasto chinari dinchha. sharir le pahile nai ladne shakti (antibody) banauchha, ra pachhi sakkali kitanu aauda chhitai ladchha.

### knowledge
U: depression bhaneko k ho?
N: depression bhaneko dherai hapta samma lagatar udas hune, man parne kura ma ruchi harauna, nidra ra bhok badalinne mansik rog ho. yo kamjori haina, upachar sambhab chha, tyasaile bharosa ko manchhe ra doctor sanga kura garnus.

### knowledge
U: anxiety (chinta rog) ka lakshan k hun?
N: anxiety ma dherai chinta lagne, mutu dhadkine, sas chhito chalne, pasina aaune ra nidra nalagne hunchha. bistarai gahiro sas line abhyas, byayam ra paramarshadata (counsellor) sanga kura garda maddat garchha.

### knowledge
U: CPR kasari dinchhan?
N: sas nafereko manchhe ko chhati ko bich ma dubai haat rakhera prati minet 100 dekhi 120 ko gati ma jod le thichnus. talim bhaye 30 choti thichepachhi 2 choti sas dinus, ra ambulance aaunjel nachhodnus.

### knowledge

N: manchhe khokna sakchha bhane khoki rahan dinus. bolna ra sas ferna sakdaina bhane pith ma 5 choti thappad lagaunus, ani pet ma mathitira 5 choti thichnus (Heimlich), ra chahiye ambulance bolaunus.

### knowledge

N: aafu pani ma haamfalnu bhanda dori, dandi ya tairaune saman phyaknus. bahira nikalepachhi sas chhaina bhane CPR suru garnus ra turuntai ambulance bolaunus.

### knowledge

### knowledge

N: lagatar charko halla le sunne shakti ghataaune, tanab badhaune, nidra bigarne ra BP badhaune samasya lyauchha. 85 decibel bhanda charko awaj lamo samaya sunnu kaan ko lagi haanikarak maninchha.

### knowledge
U: ayurveda bhaneko k ho?
N: ayurveda prachin dakshin asia ko upachar paddhati ho, jasma jadibuti, khana ra jiwanshaili bata swasthya rakhne kura garinchha. nepal ma jadibuti dherai paaine hunda ayurveda ko lamo parampara chha.

### knowledge
U: kasai lai dudh kina pachdaina?
N: kehi manchhe ko sharir ma dudh ko chini (lactose) pachaune lactase bhanne enzyme kam banchha, tyasaile dudh khada pet fulne, gudgud garne ra pakhala lagne hunchha. dahi ma lactose kam hune hunda kehi le dahi chai pachaauna sakchhan.

### knowledge
U: tauko ma chot lagepachhi kun lakshan khatarnak hun?
N: tauko ma chot lagepachhi barambar ulti, bholau hune, behos hune, eutai aankha ko nani thulo hune ya kaan-naak bata ragat ya pani aaune bhaye turuntai aspatal jaanus. yasto lakshan kehi ghanta pachhi pani dekhina sakchhan.

### knowledge
U: haddi bhaachiyo jasto lagyo bhane k garne?
N: chot lageko bhag nachalaunus, dandi ra kapada le halka bandhera (splint) sthir rakhnus ra aspatal laijaanus. haddi chhala bahira niskeko bhaye ghau lai sapha kapada le chhopnus.

### knowledge
U: chiya ra coffee le kina nidra bhagaauchha?
N: chiya ra coffee ma caffeine hunchha, jasle dimag lai sajag banaune ra thakai ko mahasus ghataaune kaam garchha. beluka dherai piye raati nidra lagna garo hunchha.

### knowledge
U: kasai lai tadha ko kura kina dhamilo dekhinchha?
N: najik drishti dosh (myopia) ma aankha bhitra prakash retina bhanda agadi kendrit hunchha, tyasaile tadha ko kura dhamilo dekhinchha. ulto, dur drishti dosh ma najik ko kura dhamilo dekhinchha; sahi lens bhayeko chashma le dubai sapha dekhauchha.

### knowledge
U: ghaam ma dherai basda chhala kina kalo hunchha?
N: ghaam ko UV kiran bata jogauna chhala le badhi melanin banauchha, tyasaile chhala kalo hunchha. tara dherai ghaam le chhala polne ra chhala ko cancer ko khatara badhauchha.

### knowledge
U: dam (asthma) ma k hunchha?
N: dam ma sas nali sunnine ra sangurine hunda sas ferna garo hunchha ra sitti bajeko jasto awaj aauchha. inhaler le aushadhi sidhai phokso samma puryaune hunda chhitai aaram dinchha.
