### knowledge
U: bidyut pravah (electric current) bhaneko k ho?
N: bidyut pravah bhaneko taar bhitra electron ko bahav ho. yasko ekai ampere ho ra ammeter le naapinchha.

### knowledge
U: voltage bhaneko k ho?
N: voltage bhaneko bijuli lai taar bhitra dhakelne "chap" ho. yasko ekai volt ho, jastai nepal ko ghar ko bijuli 230 volt ko hunchha.

### knowledge
U: nepal ma ghar ko bijuli kati volt ko hunchha?
N: nepal ma ghar ko bijuli sadharan rup ma 230 volt ra 50 hertz ko hunchha. bideshi saman kinda yo milchha ki nai herna parchha.
G: 230

### knowledge
U: bijuli ko bill ma 1 unit bhaneko k ho?
N: bijuli ko 1 unit bhaneko 1 kilowatt-ghanta (kWh) ho. jastai 1000 watt ko heater 1 ghanta chalauda 1 unit bijuli khapat hunchha.

### knowledge
U: 100 watt ko bulb 10 ghanta balda kati unit lagchha?
T: 100 watt = 0.1 kilowatt. 0.1 x 10 = 1.
N: 100 watt ko bulb 10 ghanta balda 1 unit bijuli lagchha.
G: 1

### knowledge
U: watt bhaneko k ho?
N: watt bijuli ko shakti (power) naapne ekai ho. jati dherai watt, tyati dherai bijuli ek ghanta ma khapat hunchha.

### knowledge
U: chalak ra kuchalak bhaneko k ho?
N: chalak (conductor) le bijuli sajilai bagna dinchha, jastai tama, aluminium ra falam. kuchalak (insulator) le bijuli rokchha, jastai rubber, plastic, kath ra kaanch.

### knowledge
U: bijuli ko taar tama ko kina banaunchhan?
N: tama ma bijuli dherai sajilai bagchha ra yo chaandi bhanda sasto pani chha. tyasaile ghar ko taar dherai jaso tama ko hunchha ra mathi plastic le chhopiyeko hunchha.

### knowledge
U: sabai bhanda ramro bijuli chalak dhatu kun ho?
N: sabai bhanda ramro bijuli chalak dhatu chaandi ho. tara mahango bhayeko le taar ma tama prayog garinchha.

### knowledge
U: bhijeko haat le switch kina chhunu hudaina?
N: pani ma ghuleko nun ra fohor le bijuli bagna dinchha, ra bhijeko chhala le bijuli sajilai bhitra pasna dinchha. tyasaile bhijeko haat le switch chhuda current lagne khatara badhchha.

### knowledge
U: current lagyo bhane k garne?
N: current lageko manchhe lai sidhai nachhunus. pahila main switch band garnus ya sukkha kath jasto kuchalak le bijuli ko taar hataunus, ani matra maddat garnus ra aspatal laijaanus.

### knowledge
U: fuse ra MCB ko kaam k ho?
N: fuse ra MCB le taar ma dherai current bagda bijuli aafai kaatidinchha. yasle taar tatera aago lagne ra saman bigrine bata jogauchha.

### knowledge
U: earthing kina garinchha?
N: earthing le saman ma chuhiyeko bijuli lai taar bata jamin samma lagchha. yasle saman chhunda current lagne khatara kam garchha.

### knowledge
U: AC ra DC bijuli ma k farak?
N: AC (alternating current) ma bijuli ko disha barambar badalinchha, ghar ko bijuli AC ho. DC (direct current) ma bijuli eutai disha ma bagchha, battery ko bijuli DC ho.

### knowledge
U: battery le bijuli kasari dinchha?
N: battery bhitra rasaayanik padartha hunchhan, jasko pratikriya le bijuli banchha. rasaayanik urja bijuli urja ma badalinchha.

### knowledge
U: circuit bhaneko k ho?
N: circuit bhaneko bijuli bagne pura bato ho: battery ya srot, taar ra bulb jasta saman. bato kahi tuteko bhaye (open circuit) bijuli bagdaina.

### knowledge
U: ghar ma saman haru parallel circuit ma kina jodinchhan?
N: parallel circuit ma harek saman le pura voltage paauchha ra eutai saman bigriye pani aru chaliraha hunchhan. tyasaile ghar ka bulb ra saman parallel ma jodinchhan.

### knowledge
U: ohm ko niyam k ho?
N: ohm ko niyam anusar voltage barabar current gunda resistance hunchha (V = I x R). resistance badhe current ghatchha.

### knowledge
U: resistance bhaneko k ho?
N: resistance bhaneko bijuli ko bahav lai rokne gun ho. yasko ekai ohm ho; patalo ra lamo taar ma resistance badhi hunchha.

### knowledge
U: LED bulb kina ramro?
N: LED bulb le purano bulb bhanda dherai kam bijuli khanchha ra dherai barsa chalchha. yasle garmi pani kam nikalchha.

### knowledge
U: bijuli ko bulb kasle banaye?
N: lamo samaya balne bijuli ko bulb thomas edison le 1879 ma banaye. unbhanda pahila pani kehi bigyan haru le bulb ma kaam gareka thiye.

### knowledge
U: static bijuli bhaneko k ho?
N: static bijuli bhaneko chij haru ragadda jamma hune bijuli ho. balloon lai kapal ma ragadda balloon le kapal tanchha, yo static bijuli ko udaharan ho.

### knowledge
U: chatyang pani bijuli nai ho?
N: ho, chatyang badal ma jamma bhayeko static bijuli ko ekdam thulo chingari ho. yo dherai shaktishali ra khatarnak hunchha.

### knowledge
U: solar panel le kasari bijuli banauchha?
N: solar panel ma bhayeka silicon ka koshika ma ghaam ko prakash pardaa electron chalchhan ra bijuli banchha. yasma dhuwa ra aawaj hudaina.

### knowledge
U: generator le kasari bijuli banauchha?
N: generator ma taar ko kundali chumbak ko bich ghumauda bijuli banchha. jalvidyut ma bagne pani le ra diesel generator ma engine le yo kundali ghumauchha.

### knowledge
U: chumbak le kun kun dhatu tanchha?
N: chumbak le falam, nickel ra cobalt jasta dhatu tanchha, ra falam bata baneko steel lai pani. tama, aluminium, kaagaj ra plastic lai tandaina.

### knowledge
U: chumbak ka kati dhruva hunchhan?
N: harek chumbak ka 2 dhruva hunchhan: uttari dhruva ra dakshini dhruva. ekai dhruva ek arka lai dhakelchhan ra farak dhruva tanchhan.
G: 2

### knowledge
U: chumbak lai tukra parda k hunchha?
N: chumbak lai tukra parda harek tukra afai euta saano pura chumbak banchha, jasma uttari ra dakshini dubai dhruva hunchhan. eutai dhruva matra bhayeko chumbak banna sakdaina.

### knowledge
U: compass ko suiro kina uttar tira farkinchha?
N: prithvi aafai euta thulo chumbak jastai ho. compass ko saano chumbak ko suiro prithvi ko chumbakiya kshetra sanga milera uttar-dakshin disha ma rokinchha.

### knowledge
U: bidyut chumbak (electromagnet) bhaneko k ho?
N: falam ko dulo waripari taar berera bijuli pathauda baneko chumbak lai bidyut chumbak bhaninchha. bijuli band garda yasko chumbakatwa harauchha, tyasaile yaslai crane, ghanti ra motor ma prayog garinchha.

### knowledge
U: chumbak lai tatauda k hunchha?
N: chumbak lai dherai tatauda yasko chumbakiya shakti ghatchha ra harauna pani sakchha. chumbak lai pitne ya khasalne garda pani shakti kamjor hunchha.

### knowledge
U: gurutwakarshan bhaneko k ho?
N: gurutwakarshan bhaneko sabai bastu le ek arka lai tanne shakti ho. prithvi ko gurutwakarshan le garda hamle khasaleko chij tala khaschha.

### knowledge
U: newton ka gati ka niyam kati wata chhan?
N: newton ka gati ka 3 niyam chhan: (1) bahira bata bal nalage bastu ko awastha badaldaina, (2) bal = mass x acceleration, (3) harek kriya ko barabar ra ulto pratikriya hunchha.
G: 3

### knowledge
U: bus achanak rokinda hami kina agadi dhalkinchhau?
N: bus rokinda pani hamro sharir le gati ma rahirahane gun (jadatwa) le agadi jana khojchha. tyasaile achanak brake lagauda hami agadi dhalkinchhau.

### knowledge
U: rocket kasari mathi jaanchha?
N: rocket le tala tira dherai chhito gyas phyakchha ra newton ko tesro niyam anusar gyas le rocket lai ulto disha, arthat mathi dhakelchha.

### knowledge
U: gharshan (friction) ko fayada k chha?
N: gharshan le hamilai chiplana bata jogauchha, jasle hidna ra gadi ko tyre lai bato samatna maddat garchha. brake le pani gharshan le nai gadi rokchha.

### knowledge
U: chiplo bhui ma kina lada chhitai hunchha?
N: chiplo bhui ma pani ya tel le gharshan ghataunchha. khutta le bhui lai ramrari samatna nasakda manchhe chiplera ladchha.

### knowledge
U: jahaj pani ma kina dubdaina?
N: jahaj ko aakar le dherai pani hataunchha, ra hataieko pani le mathi dhakelne bal (uplavan bal) dinchha. jahaj ko tauil yo bal bhanda kam bhaye jahaj tairinchha.

### knowledge
U: falam ko kaanti pani ma dubchha tara falam ko jahaj kina tairinchha?
N: kaanti saano ra thos hunchha, tyasaile kam pani hataunchha ra dubchha. jahaj bhitra khokro ra hawa le bhariyeko hunchha, tyasaile dherai pani hataayera tairinchha.

### knowledge
U: saral yantra kati wata chhan?
N: saral yantra 6 wata chhan: lever, pulley, wheel and axle, bhiralo satah (ramp), wedge ra screw. yinle kaam garna sajilo banauchhan.
G: 6

### knowledge

N: lever ko udaharan hun: dhiki-dhiki (see-saw), kainchi, nalchhuri ra dhunga uthauna prayog garne lamo dandi. yasma saano bal le thulo bhari uthauna sakinchha.

### knowledge
U: pulley kaha prayog hunchha?

### knowledge

### knowledge
U: pratidhwani (echo) kasari sunninchha?
N: aawaj kunai thulo kada satah, jastai pahad ya bhitta ma thokkiyera farkera aauda pratidhwani sunninchha. ghati ma karaauda yo spashta sunninchha.

### knowledge
U: prakash kun disha ma yatra garchha?

### knowledge
U: aina (mirror) le kasari chhabi dekhauchha?
N: aina ko chilo satah le prakash lai farkauchha (paravartan). farkeko prakash hamro aankha ma pugda aina ma hamro chhabi dekhinchha.

### knowledge
U: pani ma rakheko dandi bango kina dekhinchha?
N: prakash pani bata hawa ma niskanda yasko disha ali badalinchha, yaslai apavartan bhaninchha. tyasaile pani ma rakheko dandi bhaachiye jasto bango dekhinchha.

### knowledge

### knowledge
U: tatauda dhatu kina phailinchha?

### knowledge

N: garmi 3 tarika le sarchha: sanchalan (conduction), sanbahan (convection) ra bikiran (radiation). taato bhaado ko haandi ko dandi tatnu sanchalan, umaleko pani ghumnu sanbahan ra ghaam ko garmi hamisamma aaunu bikiran ho.

### knowledge
U: taato hawa kina mathi jaanchha?

### knowledge

### knowledge
U: urja nasta hunchha?
N: urja nasta hudaina ra naya pani banna sakdaina, matra eutai rup bata arko rup ma badalinchha. jastai bulb ma bijuli urja prakash ra garmi ma badalinchha.

### knowledge

### knowledge

### knowledge
U: bijuli ko taar ma basne charaa lai kina current lagdaina?

N: mass bhaneko bastu ma bhayeko padartha ko matra ho, jun kahi gaye pani badaldaina. weight bhaneko gurutwakarshan le tanne bal ho, tyasaile jun ma weight ghatchha tara mass ustai rahanchha.

### knowledge
U: 1 kilo kapas ra 1 kilo falam kun gahrungo?
N: dubai barabar gahrungo hunchhan, kinabhane dubai 1 kilo nai ho. falam saano dekhinchha ra kapas thulo, tara tauil ustai hunchha.

### knowledge
U: barf pagalda kati tapkram hunchha?
N: barf 0 degree celsius ma paglinchha. pagaldai garda pura barf pagalinjel tapkram 0 degree mai rahanchha.

### knowledge
U: nun le barf lai kina chhito paglauchha?
N: nun le pani jamne tapkram lai 0 degree bhanda tala jharauchha. tyasaile nun haleko barf 0 degree mai pani bhayera chhito paglinchha.

N: syau kaatda bhitra ko enzyme hawa ko oxygen sanga pratikriya garchha ra khairo rang banchha. kagati ko ras lagaye yo dhilo hunchha.

### knowledge
U: roti/pauroti kina phulchha?
N: pauroti ko pitho ma haleko yeast (khamir) le chini khayera carbon dioxide gyas banauchha. gyas ka fokaa pitho bhitra thunida pauroti phulera naram hunchha.

N: piyaj katda yasko koshika futera aankha lai polne gyas niskinchha. aankha le tyo gyas pakhalna aansu nikalchha.

### knowledge
U: pani ma nun ghulyo bhane kasari nikalne?
N: nun ghuleko pani lai tatayera bafa banayo bhane pani udchha ra nun bhaado ma rahanchha. samudra bata nun nikalne tarika pani yahi ho.

### knowledge
U: mishran ra yogik ma k farak?
N: mishran ma padartha haru matra misiyeka hunchhan ra sajilai chhuttyauna sakinchha, jastai nun-pani. yogik ma tatva haru rasaayanik rup le jodiyeka hunchhan, jastai pani (H2O), jun sadharan tarika le chhuttindaina.

### knowledge
U: LPG ma k gyas hunchha?
N: LPG ma mukhya rup ma propane ra butane gyas hunchha, jaslai chap dera tarala banaieko hunchha. cylinder bhitra tarala ra kholda gyas bhayera niskinchha.

### knowledge
U: CNG ra LPG ma farak k?
N: LPG propane-butane ho, jun ghar ko chulo ma dherai prayog hunchha. CNG (compressed natural gas) mukhya rup ma methane ho ra kati desh ma gadi chalauna prayog hunchha.

### knowledge
U: petrol k bata banchha?
N: petrol jamin muni bata nikaleko kachcha tel (crude oil) lai shuddha garera banainchha. yo sakine (non-renewable) indhan ho.

### knowledge
U: What does a magnet attract?
N: A magnet attracts iron, nickel, cobalt and steel. It does not attract copper, aluminium, wood, paper or plastic.

### knowledge
U: Why does a ship float?
N: A ship floats because its shape pushes aside a lot of water. The water pushes back up with a force (buoyancy) equal to the weight of that water, which can hold the ship's weight.

### knowledge
