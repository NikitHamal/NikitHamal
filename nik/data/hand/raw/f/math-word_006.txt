N: 4 hapta ma 1200 rupiya kharcha hunchha.
G: 1200

U: A school bag costs Rs 1800 and the shop gives 15% off. How much do I pay?
T: 1800 x 15 / 100 = 270. 1800 - 270 = 1530.
N: You pay Rs 1530.
G: 1530
