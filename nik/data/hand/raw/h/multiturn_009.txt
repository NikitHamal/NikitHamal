U: what is composting?
N: Composting is turning kitchen and garden waste into natural fertilizer.
U: can you explain more?
N: Food scraps like vegetable peels, tea leaves and dry leaves are collected in a pit or bin. Tiny living things in the soil break them down over 2 to 3 months. The result is dark, crumbly compost that makes soil richer, so plants grow better with less chemical fertilizer. Keep plastic, oil and bones out of it.
