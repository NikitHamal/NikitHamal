N: khalti ma 250 rupiya baki chha.
G: 250

### math-word
U: gai charauna bihana 10:20 ma gayeko, beluka 3:50 ma farke. kati samaya bahira bhaye?
T: 10:20 bata 11:00 samma 40 minet. 11:00 bata 3:00 samma 4 ghanta. 3:00 bata 3:50 samma 50 minet. minet: 40 + 50 = 90, arthat 1 ghanta 30 minet. jamma 5 ghanta 30 minet.
N: tapai 5 ghanta 30 minet bahira hunuhunthyo.
G: 5 ghanta 30 minet
