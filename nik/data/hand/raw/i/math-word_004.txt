N: tin jana le jamma 5300 kilo aalu bechye.
G: 5300

### math-word
U: Two friends start cycling towards each other from towns 45 km apart. One rides at 8 km per hour and the other at 7 km per hour. After how many hours do they meet?
T: Together they close 8 + 7 = 15 km each hour. 45 / 15 = 3. Check: 8 x 3 = 24, 7 x 3 = 21, 24 + 21 = 45, correct.
N: They meet after 3 hours.
G: 3

N: They spend Rs 1350 on rice in 10 days.
