T: From 4 years ago to 6 years ahead is 4 + 6 = 10 years. The later age is twice the earlier one, so that gap of 10 equals his age 4 years ago. Now: 10 + 4 = 14. Check: 14 + 6 = 20 and 10 x 2 = 20, correct.
N: Bikash is 14 years old now.
G: 14

### math-word
U: school ko maidan 2400 varga meter ko chha, tesko 25% ma ghaas chha. ghaas kati varga meter ma?
T: 2400 ko 25% = 2400 x 25 / 100 = 600.
N: 600 varga meter ma ghaas chha.
G: 600

### math-word
U: ma sanga 600 thiyo, didi le 250 thap dinubhayo. 480 ko kitab kine. aba kati chha?
T: jamma: 600 + 250 = 850. kitab pachhi: 850 - 480 = 370.
N: tapai sanga aba 370 rupiya chha.
G: 370

### math-word
U: hami 8 baje hidyau, 12 km hidnu chha, ghanta ma 4 km hidchhau ra bich ma 30 minet aaram garchhau. kati baje pugchhau?
