T: jamma 5 jana: 4 + 1 = 5. camera ko mol diyeko chhaina.
N: tapai haru 5 jana hunuhunchha. camera kati ko ho? mol bhannus, 5 le bhag garera ek jana ko bhag bhanidinchhu.

### math-word
U: khurkot ko pasal ma chait ma 3578 ra baisakh ma 2649 rupiya ko bikri bhayo. dui mahina ko jamma?
T: 3578 + 2649 thau anusar: ek ko thau: 8 + 9 = 17, 7 lekhne, 1 haat. das ko thau: 7 + 4 + 1 = 12, 2 lekhne, 1 haat. saya ko thau: 5 + 6 + 1 = 12, 2 lekhne, 1 haat. hajar ko thau: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. jamma 6227. jaach: 6227 - 2649 = 3578, milyo.
N: dui mahina ko jamma bikri 6227 rupiya.
G: 6227

### math-word
U: A shop has 250 items, and 80 of them are shirts. 20% of the shirts are on sale. How many shirts are on sale?
T: 80 x 20 / 100 = 16. The 250 items are not needed.
N: 16 shirts are on sale.
G: 16

### math-word
U: bahini ko gullak ma 1340 thiyo. usle dui palta 250 ra 180 kharcha garin. aba kati baki?
T: kharcha: 250 + 180 = 430. baki: 1340 - 430 = 910.
N: bahini ko gullak ma aba 910 rupiya baki chha.
G: 910

### math-word

### math-word
U: 3 kilo pyaj 255 ma ra 5 kilo 400 ma painchha. kun linda kilo ko sasto?
T: 3 kilo ma: 255 / 3 = 85. 5 kilo ma: 400 / 5 = 80.
N: 5 kilo linda sasto, kilo ko 80 rupiya parchha (3 kilo ma 85).
G: 5 kilo
