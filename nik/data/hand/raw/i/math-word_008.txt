U: 3 mahina ko bachat 2450, 1875 ra 2690. jamma kati?
T: 2450 + 1875 + 2690 thau anusar: ek ko thau: 0 + 5 + 0 = 5, 5 lekhne. das ko thau: 5 + 7 + 9 = 21, 1 lekhne, 2 haat. saya ko thau: 4 + 8 + 6 + 2 = 20, 0 lekhne, 2 haat. hajar ko thau: 2 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 7. jamma 7015. jaach: 2450 + 1875 = 4325, 4325 + 2690 = 7015, milyo.

### math-word
U: kotha bhada 8000 bata 8800 bhayo. kati pratishat badhyo?

N: bhada 10 pratishat badhyo.
G: 10

### math-word
U: Two cyclists start together on the same road at 12 km per hour and 15 km per hour. How far apart are they after 2 hours 20 minutes?
T: The gap grows by 15 - 12 = 3 km each hour. In 2 hours: 3 x 2 = 6 km. In the next 20 minutes, a third of an hour: 3 / 3 = 1 km. Total: 6 + 1 = 7 km.
N: They are 7 km apart.
G: 7

### math-word
