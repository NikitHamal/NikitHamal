Conventions: unused number -> `... yo hisab ma chahindaina.` (en: `... is not needed.`); missing number -> `T: ... diyeko
chhaina.` + N asks for that one value, no G. Clock times never touch `=` (`6:50 bata 10 minet ma 7:00 ...`); no unit
words inside an equation (`200 / 20 = 10 tile`, never `200 cm / 20 = 10`); remainders: `47 lai 5 le bhag garda 9
aauchha, baki 2` + N says where the remainder goes (last row, extra jeep, juice, kept at home).
