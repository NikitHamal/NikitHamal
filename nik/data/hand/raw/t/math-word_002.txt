N: hapta ma din ko ausat 2 ghanta padhnubhayo.

### math-word
U: A school buys 6 boxes of chalk with 24 sticks in each box. Each of its 4 classrooms uses 9 sticks a week. How many weeks will the chalk last?
T: Total sticks: 6 x 24 = 144. Used per week: 4 x 9 = 36. Weeks: 144 / 36 = 4.
N: The chalk will last 4 weeks.
G: 4
