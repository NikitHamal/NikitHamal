U: How can you find the height of a tree using its shadow?

U: What is an astronomical unit (AU)?
N: An astronomical unit is the average distance from the Earth to the sun, about 150 million km. Astronomers use it to describe distances inside the solar system; Jupiter, for example, is about 5 AU from the sun.

U: Why do most rockets launch towards the east?
N: The Earth spins from west to east, so a rocket launched eastward gets a free boost from that spin. Launch sites near the equator get the biggest boost, because the ground there moves fastest.

### knowledge
