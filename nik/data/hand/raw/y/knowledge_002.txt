### knowledge
U: What is the difference between an allergy and an infection?
N: In an allergy, the body overreacts to something harmless, like dust, pollen or a food, as if it were an enemy. In an infection, germs really get into the body and cause illness; an allergy cannot spread to other people.

U: What is the difference between a crocodile and an alligator?
N: A crocodile has a narrow, V-shaped snout, and its lower teeth still show when its mouth is shut. An alligator has a wide, U-shaped snout with the lower teeth hidden, and alligators live only in the USA and China.

### knowledge
U: What is the difference between an electric motor and a generator?
N: A motor uses electricity to make something turn, like a fan or a water pump. A generator does the opposite: something turns it, like flowing water or an engine, and it makes electricity.

### knowledge
U: What is a semiconductor, and how is it different from a conductor?
N: A conductor like copper lets electricity flow easily all the time. A semiconductor like silicon lets only a little flow, and that flow can be switched and controlled, which is why it is used in chips, LEDs and solar cells.

### knowledge

N: An atom is the tiny basic particle that matter is made of. An element is a substance made of only one kind of atom, like gold, oxygen or carbon; there are 118 known elements.

U: What is the difference between an alloy and a pure metal?

N: In a solid, the particles are packed tightly and only shake in place, so a solid keeps its shape. In a liquid, the particles are still close but can slide past each other, so a liquid flows and takes the shape of its container.
