N: The oldest surviving photograph from a camera was made in France in the 1820s by Nicephore Niepce. It needed many hours of light, while today a phone takes a photo in a fraction of a second.

U: When did people first fly in a hot air balloon?
N: In 1783 in France, a hot air balloon made by the Montgolfier brothers carried the first people into the sky. Earlier the same year they had sent up a sheep, a duck and a rooster.

### knowledge
U: Who built the first practical helicopter?
N: Igor Sikorsky, an engineer born in Kyiv who worked in the USA, flew his first practical helicopter in 1939. His single main rotor with a small tail rotor is still the layout most helicopters use.

### knowledge

N: Robert Goddard of the USA, in 1926. It flew for only about two and a half seconds, but rockets that later went to space were built on the same idea.

### knowledge
U: Who first proposed that everything is made of atoms, in modern science?
N: In the early 1800s, English chemist John Dalton put forward the modern atomic theory: each element is made of its own kind of tiny atoms. The idea of atoms is much older, from ancient Greek and Indian thinkers.

### knowledge
U: Who discovered the electron?
N: J. J. Thomson in England discovered the electron in 1897. It was the first particle found that is smaller than an atom.

### knowledge
U: Who discovered radioactivity?
N: Henri Becquerel in France discovered it in 1896, when uranium salts darkened a photographic plate in a closed drawer. Marie and Pierre Curie then studied it further and found new radioactive elements.

### knowledge
U: When was the first baby born through IVF ("test-tube baby")?
N: Louise Brown, born in England in 1978, was the first baby born through IVF. In IVF the egg and sperm meet in a lab dish, and the early embryo is then placed in the mother's womb.

### knowledge
U: What was Dolly the sheep?
N: Dolly, born in Scotland in 1996, was the first mammal cloned from a cell of an adult animal. She was a genetic copy of the sheep that gave the cell.

N: BC means "before Christ" and AD means "Anno Domini", years counted from the traditional birth year of Jesus. BCE ("before the common era") and CE ("common era") use exactly the same years, just with names that are not religious.
