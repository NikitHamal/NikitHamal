U: Why should you crawl low when a room fills with smoke?
N: Hot smoke and poisonous gases rise and collect near the ceiling first. The air close to the floor stays cooler and cleaner for longer, so crawling low helps you breathe and see the way out.

### knowledge
U: Why do newborn babies lose body heat so quickly?
N: A baby has a large skin surface for its small body and very little fat to keep warm. That is why newborns are dried at once, wrapped well, kept on the mother's chest and given a cap.

### knowledge
U: Why does wet sand stick together but dry sand pours away?
N: In damp sand, a thin film of water between the grains pulls them together, so you can shape it. Dry sand has no water bridges, and very wet sand has too much water, so both fall apart.

### knowledge
U: Why do we get more small electric shocks from door handles in dry winter?
N: Walking and rubbing against clothes builds up static charge on the body. Damp air slowly leaks that charge away, but dry winter air does not, so it jumps to metal as a little spark.

### knowledge
U: Why does the ground still feel warm in the evening after a sunny day?
N: Soil, stone and concrete soak up the sun's heat during the day and release it slowly. So the ground and walls stay warm for hours after sunset and give that heat back to the air.
