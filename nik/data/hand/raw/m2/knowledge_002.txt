### knowledge

### knowledge

N: A warm-blooded animal keeps its body temperature steady using heat from its own food, whether the weather is hot or cold. Mammals and birds are warm-blooded; fish, frogs and reptiles are not.

### knowledge
U: Which is the largest reptile alive today?
N: The saltwater crocodile is the largest living reptile. Big males can grow longer than a car and live in rivers and coasts from India to Australia.

### knowledge
U: Which snake is the longest in the world?
N: The reticulated python of Southeast Asia is the longest snake. The green anaconda of South America is shorter but heavier.

### knowledge
U: How do bats sleep?
N: Bats sleep hanging upside down by their feet in caves, trees or roofs. Their feet lock in place when they rest, so they do not fall, and from that position they can drop and fly off quickly.

### knowledge
U: Why do some animals play dead?
N: Many predators only eat prey that is moving or freshly killed. Animals like the opossum and some snakes and beetles lie still as if dead, and the attacker often loses interest and leaves.

### knowledge
U: How do camels walk on hot sand?
N: Camels have wide, flat feet with thick, tough pads that spread out on the sand so they do not sink. The pads also protect their feet from the hot ground.

### knowledge
U: Why do giraffes have such long necks?
N: A long neck lets a giraffe reach leaves high in the trees that other animals cannot reach. Male giraffes also swing their necks at each other when they fight.

### knowledge
U: How do polar bears hunt?
N: Polar bears mostly hunt seals on the sea ice. A bear waits beside a hole in the ice where seals come up to breathe and grabs one when it appears.

### knowledge
U: Why do squirrels bury nuts?
N: Many squirrels bury nuts and seeds in autumn to eat later when food is scarce. They forget some of them, and those seeds can grow into new trees.

### knowledge
U: How do birds know when to migrate?
N: Birds notice the days getting shorter or longer and the weather changing, which tells their bodies it is time to move. Many young birds also follow older birds on their first journey.

### knowledge
U: Do fish drink water?
N: Sea fish do drink seawater, and they get rid of the extra salt through their gills. River and lake fish hardly drink at all, because water keeps soaking into their bodies through their skin and gills.

### knowledge
U: How do eagles see prey from so high up?
N: Eagles have much sharper eyesight than people, with far more light-sensing cells packed into the back of the eye. From high in the sky they can spot a small animal moving in the grass.

### knowledge
U: Why do mosquitoes make a buzzing sound?
N: The buzz is the sound of a mosquito's wings beating hundreds of times every second. Male and female mosquitoes even buzz at different pitches.
