U: Which part of the cinnamon plant is the spice?
N: Cinnamon is the dried inner bark of the cinnamon tree. Strips of bark are peeled off and dried, and they curl up into the rolls we buy.

U: What part of a plant is black pepper?
N: Black pepper is the dried fruit of a climbing vine. The small green berries are picked and dried in the sun until they turn black and wrinkled.

N: The banana flower, the large purple bud at the end of the bunch, is cooked as a vegetable. The soft inner core of the stem is also cooked in some places.

### knowledge
U: Why does a rooster crow in the morning?
N: A rooster has an inner body clock that tells it when morning is coming, so it crows around dawn. It also crows at other times to mark its area and call to other chickens.

### knowledge
U: Why do buffaloes like to lie in mud and water?
N: Buffaloes have few sweat glands, so they cannot cool down well by sweating. Lying in water or mud keeps them cool and also protects their skin from flies.

### knowledge

### knowledge
U: Why does water roll off a duck's back?
N: A duck spreads oil from a gland near its tail over its feathers. The oily feathers keep water out, so the duck stays dry and warm while swimming.

### knowledge

### knowledge

### knowledge

U: tuin bhaneko k ho?

U: asare geet kahile gainchha?

### knowledge
U: hiundo ma tusaro le tarkari kina marchha?

### knowledge
U: How do farmers protect seedlings from frost?
N: Farmers cover young plants with straw, plastic or dry leaves on cold nights. The cover keeps the warmth of the soil around the plants, so ice does not form on the leaves.

### knowledge
U: chiya ko bot lai kina chhoto katchhan?
N: chiya ko bot lai chhoto katda (pruning) dherai naya komal munta aauchhan. tipne manchhe ko haat pugne uchai ma bot rahanchha, tyasaile tipna pani sajilo hunchha.

### knowledge
U: What is terrace farming?
N: Terrace farming is growing crops on flat steps cut into a hillside. The steps slow down rainwater, stop the soil from washing away and make the land easier to plough.

### knowledge
U: Why do farmers leave a field empty for a season sometimes?
N: Leaving a field to rest, called fallowing, lets the soil recover some of its nutrients and moisture. Weeds and pests that depend on the crop also become fewer.

### knowledge
U: What is the difference between a tractor and a power tiller?
N: A tractor is a large four-wheeled machine that pulls heavy ploughs and trailers. A power tiller is a smaller two-wheeled machine that a person walks behind, which suits small terraced fields.

### knowledge
U: barsat ma bari ko mato kina bagchha?
N: thulo pani parda pani le mato ko mathillo tah bagaidinchha, khas gari bhiralo ra nango jamin ma. ghans, rukh ra gara le pani ko gati dhilo bhaera mato jogainchha.

### knowledge
U: What is mulching?
N: Mulching is covering the soil around plants with straw, dry leaves or grass. It keeps the soil moist, stops weeds from growing and protects the soil from heavy rain.

### knowledge
U: What is a greenhouse used for in farming?
N: A farm greenhouse is a frame covered with clear plastic or glass that traps the sun's warmth. Farmers in cold places use it to grow tomatoes and vegetables earlier and longer in the year.

### knowledge
U: plastic ghar (tunnel) ma tarkari kina chhito hurkinchha?
N: plastic ghar bhitra ghaam ko taap bhitrai thunninchha ra bahira bhanda nyano hunchha. tusaro ra thulo pani bata pani biruwa jogiyera chhito hurkinchha.

### knowledge
U: What is grafting in fruit trees?
N: Grafting is joining a cut shoot from a good fruit tree onto the stem and roots of another plant so they grow as one. In Nepali a grafted plant is called "kalami", and oranges and apples are often grown this way.

### knowledge
U: dhan ko khet ma machha palna milchha?
N: milchha, dhan khet ma pani jamne bhayeko le kati kisan le dhan sangai machha palchhan. machha le jhaar ra kira khanchhan ra tiniharu ko mal le dhan lai fayada garchha.

### knowledge
U: Why do potatoes turn green, and can we eat the green part?
N: Potatoes kept in light make green chlorophyll, and at the same time a bitter natural poison builds up in the green part. Cut the green part and any sprouts away before cooking, and store potatoes in a dark place.

### knowledge
U: What does a honeybee's dance in the hive mean?
N: A bee that finds good flowers does a "waggle dance" when it returns to the hive. The direction and length of the dance tell the other bees which way and how far to fly to reach the food.

### knowledge
U: mah kina jamera daanaa daanaa hunchha?
N: mah ma dherai guliyo hunchha, ra chiso ma tyo guliyo saana daana (crystal) ma jamna thalchha. yo bigreko haina; mah lai halka nyano pani ma rakhda pheri patalo hunchha.

### knowledge
U: kisan le bari ma kharani kina chharchhan?
N: kaath ko kharani ma potash (potassium) ra calcium jasta tatva hunchhan, jun biruwa lai chahinchha. yasle amilo mato ko amilopan pani ghataidinchha.

### knowledge
U: Why must kidney beans be cooked fully?
N: Raw or undercooked kidney beans (rajma) contain a natural toxin that can cause stomach pain and vomiting. Soaking them and then boiling them well until soft destroys it.

### knowledge
U: What is organic farming?
N: Organic farming grows crops without factory-made fertilizers and pesticides. Farmers use compost, animal manure, crop rotation and natural ways of controlling pests instead.

### knowledge
U: Why are many old village houses in the hills painted with red mud and white clay?
N: Village houses are often plastered with red clay on the lower part and white clay above. The mud plaster protects the walls from rain and dust, and freshly plastering the house is a common way to make it look clean for festivals like Dashain and Tihar.

### knowledge
U: maato le ghar ko bhui lipda k fayada hunchha?
N: maato ra gobar le lipeko bhui chiplo hudaina ra dhulo kam udchha. pahile gaun ma yo nai bhui safa ra samtal rakhne sajilo upaya thiyo.

### knowledge
U: What is a bullock cart?
N: A bullock cart is a two-wheeled wooden cart pulled by a pair of oxen. In some Terai villages it is still used to carry harvests, firewood and goods along farm roads.

### knowledge
U: Why do some farmers keep ducks in their rice fields?
N: Ducks swim between the rice plants and eat insects, snails and small weeds. Their droppings also feed the soil, so the farmer needs fewer chemicals.

### knowledge
U: How do farmers know rice is ready to harvest?
N: Rice is ready when most of the grains on the heads have turned golden and hard and the heads bend down with their weight. If it is cut too late, many grains fall off in the field.

### knowledge
U: What is the difference between a sheep and a goat?
N: Sheep usually have thick wool and graze on grass close to the ground, and their tails hang down. Goats have hair instead of wool, like to nibble leaves and bushes, and their tails usually point up.

### knowledge
U: Why does a cow give milk only after having a calf?
N: Like all mammals, a cow makes milk to feed her young, so milk starts after she gives birth. She keeps giving milk for several months, and the amount slowly drops over time.

### knowledge
U: Can a hen lay eggs without a rooster?
N: Yes. A hen lays eggs whether or not there is a rooster, but those eggs are not fertilized and will never hatch into chicks.

### knowledge
U: What do bees collect from flowers besides nectar?
N: Bees also collect pollen, which they carry on their back legs. Pollen is protein food for the young bees, while nectar becomes honey.

### knowledge
U: Why are the cells in a honeycomb six-sided?
N: Six-sided cells fit together with no gaps between them. This shape stores the most honey while using the least wax, and it makes the comb strong.

### knowledge
U: Why do farmers dry grain in the sun before storing it?
N: Damp grain gets mouldy, starts to rot and attracts insects. Drying it well in the sun lets it be stored safely for many months.

### knowledge
U: Why do hill farmers hang maize cobs under the roof?
N: Hanging the cobs in bundles under the roof lets them dry in the air while keeping them out of the rain and off the damp ground. It also makes it harder for rats to reach them.
