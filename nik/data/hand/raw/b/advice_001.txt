N: chad najik aaunda ticket chhito sakinchha, 2-3 hapta agadi nai online ki counter bata kinnus. sakchhu bhane bheed suru hunu agadi nai yatra garnus. aadhikarik counter matra prayog garnus, bato ma badhi mol ma ticket bechne lai bharosa nagarnus. yatra ko din pani, charge ra khaja sangai rakhnus.

### advice
U: deusi bhailo ma kati paisa uthauda team ma kasari baadne?
N: suru mai niyam banaunus: uthayeko paisa ek jana sanga rakhne ra har raat hisab lekhne. kharcha, jastai khaja ra baja ko bhada, pahila chhuttyaunus. baki paisa baraabar baadne ki samaj ko kaam ma lagaune bhanne pahila nai sahamati garnus. spasta hisab le sathi bich jhagada hudaina.

### advice
U: monsoon ma ghar ma pani pasne dar
N: barsha agadi nai ghar ko chhana ra nali sapha garnus, pani bagne bato khulla rakhnus. mahatwapurna kagajpatra plastic ma bandera uchai ma rakhnus. bijuli ko saman ra switch bhuin bata tadha rakhnus. pahiro ki baadhi ko khatara bhayeko thau ma basnu hunchha bhane bhaagne bato ra najik ko surakshit thau pahila nai thaha paunus.

### advice
U: bhuichalo aayo bhane k garne?
N: bhitra hunuhunchha bhane "jhuk, chhop, samat": bhuin ma jhuknus, baliyo table muni taauko chhopnus ra samatnus. jhyal, sisa ra dhalna sakne saman bata tadha rahanus. hallauna banda bhayepachhi bistarai khulla thau ma jaanus, lift prayog nagarnus. ghar ma pani, torch ra first aid bhayeko sano jhola pahila nai tayar rakhnus.

### advice
U: aago lagyo bhane k garne?
N: sano aago bhaye bhijeko bora ki baluwa le nibhaunus, tel ko aago ma pani nahalnus. aago thulo bhaye sabai lai bahira niklanus ra 101 ma phone garnus. dhuwa ma tala jhukera hidnus, mathi dhuwa badhi hunchha. lugaa ma aago lagyo bhane nadaudnus, bhuin ma ladera nibhaunus.

### advice
U: pahilo palta job ma, sabai senior, dar lagchha
N: naya hunda dar lagnu swabhabik ho, sabai le kahile na kahile yo bhogeka hunchhan. thaha nabhayeko kura sodhna nadarnus, "ma sikdai chhu" bhanne kura sabai le bujhchhan. aafno kaam samaya mai ra dhyan dera garnus, bharosa bistarai banchha. ek jana bharosa garna milne senior lai salah dine sathi banaunus.

### advice
U: kaam chhodne ki nachhodne, confuse chhu
N: afno karan lekhnus: kina chhodna man chha ra kina basna man chha. samasya sudharna milne chha ki, jastai manager sanga kura garera, tyo pahila hernus. arko kaam napai chhodnu bhanda pahila arko khojnus, aamdani rokinchha. 3-6 mahina ko kharcha bachat bhaye nirnaya garna sajilo hunchha.

### advice
U: mero manager le sabai credit afai linchha
N: afno kaam ko record rakhnus: k garyo, kahile, k upalabdhi bhayo, email ma pani. meeting ma afno kaam ko update afai spasta bhannus. shanta bhayera manager sanga eklai kura garnus: "yo project ma maile gareko kaam pani team le thaha paos bhanne chahanchhu." samasya badhyo bhane HR sanga salah linus.
